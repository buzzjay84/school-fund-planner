## School Fund Planner 0.1.0

First installer release.

- **Budget setup**: school details, NEP and MOOE categories, realignment between expenses.
- **Monthly SOB**: plan items per month against the monthly release (NEP ÷ 12), remove an expense from a month when nothing is planned in it, and see the month's available funds at the bottom while you scroll.
- **Quarterly APP**: items grouped by quarter.
- **Reports**: download the SOB (MOOE alignment + JAN to DEC sheets) and the APP as Excel, in the school workbook layout with working formulas. Signatories and district print at the bottom.
- **Updates**: Help > Check for Updates.

### Install
- **Windows**: download `School-Fund-Planner-Setup-0.1.0.exe` and run it.
- **macOS**: download the `.dmg` for your Mac (`arm64` for Apple silicon, `x64` for Intel). The app is not signed, so the first time right-click it and choose **Open**.
